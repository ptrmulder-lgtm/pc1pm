01 · CW decoder

Mz Cw Reader

In development

A CW decoder for Windows, built as a successor to the classic CwGet. Morse from the sound card turns straight into text, and in the spectrum and waterfall you click a signal to tune to it.

  • Sound card, file or demoLive from the sound card, from a WAV, MP3 or FLAC recording, or with a generated practice QSO.
  • Tunable filtersFIR or IIR filter with adjustable bandwidth around the tone, from very narrow to wide.
  • AFC and squelchFollows a drifting signal automatically. Squelch and SNR meter in dB above the noise.
  • Spectrum and waterfallSee every signal in the passband and click one to tune to it.
  • QSO recognitionPicks out call, RST, name, QTH, locator and power from the text automatically.
  • Logbook and ADIFKeep track of contacts and export them to your own logging program.

In tests with a synthetic 20 WPM signal it decodes cleanly at +3 dB signal-to-noise ratio (140 Hz filter) and is still readable at 0 dB (30 Hz filter).
