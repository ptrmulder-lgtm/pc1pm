01 · About me
Welcome!

Thanks for looking me up. I've been a licensed operator since June 2016, but long before that I spent many hours listening on the HF bands. Today you'll find me on HF in CW, SSB and digital modes (FT8/FT4, RTTY and SSTV), and I really enjoy a good contest – whether from home or with a team at a field day.

I hope to work you sometime in the future (again)! – Welkom op mijn pagina, hopelijk tot snel in de log!

PACC Contest – Multi-Two High Mixed as PA7AL

24 hours of contesting, 9 Feb 12:00 UTC till 10 Feb 12:00 UTC.

Team: Alfred PA7AL, Robert PA2RCW, Jos PA3HGX, Peter PD0FGQ, Andries PE1PIX, Douwe PA7D and Peter PC1PM.
